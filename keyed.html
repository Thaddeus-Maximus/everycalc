Key shear τ = F/(w·Leff), allowable 0.577·Sy. Bearing σ = F/(contact height·Leff), allowable Sy of the weaker part; contact height is t1 on the shaft side and h − t1 on the hub side. F = 2T/D at the shaft surface. Shaft: nominal stresses on full D, von Mises with Kf, modified Goodman (Shigley ch. 7). Keyseat Kt: Roark Table 6-1 (end-milled, bending), Shigley Table 7-1 (Kts = 3.0; sled-runner Kt = 1.7).
